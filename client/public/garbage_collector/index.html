THE CHALLENGE & THE SYSTEM

Why Sorting in Japan is Unique & Complex

Japan is celebrated globally for clean streets, but navigating 8 distinct categories, municipal designated bags, and crow prevention can be overwhelming for residents and travelers alike.

01
⚠️

The Confusion & Penalties

One piece of contaminated plastic can cause municipal collectors to place a red rejection sticker on your bag. Landlords issue warnings, and improper hazardous disposal (like lithium batteries) sparks garbage truck fires.

02
🏛️

How Japan Manages Waste

Japan separates waste into 4 core pillars: Combustible (Moeru), Non-Combustible (Moenai), Recyclable Resources (Shigen), and Large Bulky Items (Sodai Gomi), each with specific morning collection windows.
